Sign in to sync

Signing in is entirely optional. Verticordia Atlas works offline without an account, and your progress lives on this device.

  • Signing in adds no new features. It only lets the same progress follow you to another device.
  • Everything still works offline. Progress is saved on the device first, and syncs when a connection is there.
  • You can log out at any time. Your progress stays on the device.
