      Why Atlas

      Everything in Atlas is built for the way people actually play: in short evenings, across several saves, and sometimes with no signal at all.

      Atlas is a free, open source project from Verticordia. It is built to be fast, quiet, and clear about what it does with your data.

      • SERVICE WORKER · MAP TILES SERVED FROM LOCAL PACK

        Works offline Offline-first

        Map tiles, markers and screenshots ship with the site, so a map keeps working without a connection.

        STORED LOCAL IndexedDB blob storage unpacks map pyramids offline with no network roundtrips.

      • ISOLATED SAVE PROFILES · JSON EXPORT & IMPORT

        Track every playthrough Profiles

        Each save file or profile keeps its own found-tracking; you can switch difficulty and back up or restore progress by export and import.

        DATA SAFETY Standalone profiles prevent cross-save pollution with instant JSON backup.

      • MARKER SEARCH · INSTANT PRESET TOGGLES

        Show only what matters Presets

        Find any collectible instantly with fuzzy marker search, or switch saved selection presets and category toggles to reveal just the bosses, keys, or shrines you're hunting.

        TARGETED HUNT Tiered location search and instant layer presets mute clutter and pinpoint objectives.

      • CUSTOM WAYPOINTS · COMPACT SHAREABLE URLS

        Custom markers & share links Pins & Links

        Drop markers in your own color and icon, then share any spot with a compact link that carries just enough in the URL to preview while keeping notes packed in the fragment.

        ZERO-DB SHARING Personal waypoints stay in your profile and pack cleanly into deflate-raw URLs when shared.

      • MARQUEE DRAG SELECTION · BATCH MARK FOUND

        Bulk editing for big games Batch Tools

        Select many markers at once and edit them together, which helps in large worlds. Drag a box, ellipse or freehand lasso to mark whole regions found in seconds.

        FAST PROGRESS Spatial selection tools update dozens of markers simultaneously.

      • OPT-IN GOOGLE OR 26-CHAR KEY · CONFLICT-SAFE

        Optional sync across devices Opt-In

        Sign in with Google, or use a 26-character secret key, to sync your profiles between devices. Sync is optional: if two devices change the same profile, you're warned and choose which version to keep.

        USER CONTROL Content hashing ignores camera moves and lets you overwrite either side cleanly on conflict.
